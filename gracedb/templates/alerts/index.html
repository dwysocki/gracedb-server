Phone and email alerts

This is the central page for managing phone and email alerts. From here, you can create new contacts and notifications, edit or delete existing ones, and test and verify contacts.

The general procedure is:

  • Create a contact
  • Verify that contact
  • Create a notification

More detailed documentation is provided here.

Note: email alerts in ER14 and O3 will come from the gravitationalwave.services domain.
